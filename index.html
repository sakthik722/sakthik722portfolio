Case Studies

Problem → Solution → Result. Click to expand.

1 · AI-Powered Audit Automation: 70%+ less manual review

Problem

Analysts spent hours checking competitor product matches by hand. The work was slow and error-prone.

Solution

A Python pipeline that uses an LLM to check titles, attributes and identifiers, and flags only weak matches for a person to review.

Result

Manual audit time dropped by 70%+, and match accuracy went up.

2 · Competitor Product-Matching Engine

Problem

Matching competitor products to a large catalogue by hand led to missed and incorrect matches.

Solution

Matches on SKU patterns, model numbers, UPC/EAN and attributes, including SKUs hidden in page JSON.

Result

Faster, more accurate matching across thousands of products, sorted into clear tiers: exact, close and no match.

3 · Universal Shopify Scraper: full catalogue to CSV

Problem

Competitor research needed complete product data from many Shopify stores, which couldn't be copied by hand.

Solution

A reusable tool that finds the sitemap and pulls every variant. It handles unusual sitemap formats, mixed-in image links and stores that block outside requests.

Result

A whole store exports to clean CSV in one run, and it works across dozens of stores.

4 · Scraping a Bot-Protected Fashion Store

Problem

A CAPTCHA blocked requests after a few hundred product pages.

Solution

Random delays, batch pauses and safe run limits, with JSON-LD or HTML parsing as a fallback.

Result

Hundreds of products collected per run without blocks, so the whole catalogue could be gathered over several runs.

5 · KPI & SLA Dashboards with Root-Cause Analysis

Problem

There was no single view of KPIs and SLAs, so problems were spotted late.

Solution

Power BI and QuickSight dashboards built on complex SQL, with drill-downs and root-cause notes. Numbers were checked in Excel and Python first.

Result

Problems were caught before they escalated, and weekly reporting became consistent.

6 · Retail Sales Analysis with SQL

Problem

A retail business needed answers about sales trends, top products and customer behaviour.

Solution

Designed the database from scratch, explored the data and wrote complex SQL with joins, window functions and CTEs.

Result

Clear findings on revenue drivers, best-selling products and customer patterns.
